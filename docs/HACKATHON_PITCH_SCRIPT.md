## 🛠️ Step-by-Step AI Video Editing Instructions

### Step 1: Generate the Voiceover Audio
1. Open **[ElevenLabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io)**.
2. Select voice **"Adam"** or **"Marcus"** (Stability: 55%, Clarity: 85%, Speed: 1.05x).
3. Paste the clean voiceover text above.
4. Export as `soulcut_pitch_voiceover.mp3`.

### Step 2: Record Screen Actions (Screen Studio / OBS)
1. Open the live app: **[https://soulcut.onrender.com](https://soulcut.onrender.com)**.
2. Follow the 5 acts in the storyboard:
   - **Clip 1**: Hero landing page + click *"60s Judge Walkthrough"*.
   - **Clip 2**: In `/app`, click `💡 Simon Sinek (Leadership)` sample pill -> Click *"Ask your Mind"*.
   - **Clip 3**: Toggle `9:16 Framing` -> Switch between `TikTok`, `Reels`, and `Shorts`.
   - **Clip 4**: Click clip `Play` (equalizer animates) -> Open `Re-Angle Hook ▾` -> Select `🔥 Contrarian`.
   - **Clip 5**: Click `Keep` / `Not My Style` -> Show toast and jump to `/dna` to show updated confidence.
   - **Clip 6**: Click `Export Project ▾` -> Download EDL & PDF report.

### Step 3: Edit in CapCut Pro / Premiere Pro
1. Drop `soulcut_pitch_voiceover.mp3` onto Track 1.
2. Align screen recordings to match exact timestamps in the storyboard.
3. Apply auto-captions: font **Inter** or **Montserrat Bold**, size 18, color White with Lime Green (`#C7FF4B`) highlight on key action words (*Minds API*, *Creative DNA*, *EDL*, *Safe-Zones*).
4. Add whoosh and pop sound effects on each camera zoom and button click.
5. Add subtle background music at -18dB.
6. Export at **1080p 60fps (Bitrate: High, MP4)**.
